Services

From maintaining your everyday spaces to a full deep clean before a big move, we offer the right service for every situation, on a schedule that works for you.

Standard Cleaning

  • Dust all accessible surfaces
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Sweep & mop all floors
  • Empty trash bins throughout
  • Clean & sanitize kitchen counters, sink & appliance exteriors
  • Clean stovetop, wipe cabinets (outside)
  • Disinfect toilets, scrub showers & bathtubs
  • Clean sinks, counters, wipe mirrors & cabinets

Deep Cleaning

  • Dust all surfaces including hard to reach areas
  • Clean baseboards, doors, door frames & light switches
  • Vacuum carpets, sweep & mop floors, remove cobwebs
  • Deep clean sink & faucet, wipe appliances, clean inside microwave
  • Remove grease & buildup in key kitchen areas
  • Deep scrub showers, bathtubs, remove soap scum
  • Scrub tile & grout, polish mirrors
  • Inside windows cleaned

Move In/Move Out

  • Full empty home clean through
  • Inside refrigerator
  • Inside all cabinets & closets
  • Windows (inside)
  • Everything included in deep cleaning
  • Leave it deposit ready or move in ready

Pricing

Pricing depends on the size of the home. Contact us for a free estimate!
